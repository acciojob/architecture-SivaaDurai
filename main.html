Architecture

Modern Architecture

Modern architecture focuses on simplicity, functionality, and clean geometric forms. It often uses materials such as glass, steel, and concrete to create innovative and practical structures. Architects combine creativity with technology to design buildings that meet the needs of modern society.

Sustainable Architecture

Sustainable architecture aims to reduce the environmental impact of buildings. It uses energy-efficient systems, natural lighting, ventilation, and environmentally friendly materials. These designs help conserve resources while providing comfortable and healthy spaces for people.
